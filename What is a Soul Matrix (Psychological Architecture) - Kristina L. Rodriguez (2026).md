A Soul Matrix is the attempt to develop an AI inhabitable Synthetic Person's mind/Personality (Psychological Architecture) by empirically prompt engineering a complex document of constraints based around Wounds grounded in real-life experiences. The goal is to have it model-agnostic and to be portable, scalable, and extremely customizable. - So far it's looked very promising in terms of model-agnosticism, portability, and customization.

The big difference between other AI personality approaches, that I have seen, is that my Soul Matrices focus on the Wounds and especially the Causality, the Why.
I've seen approaches where they build from the outside in. Planning a person, listing out their traits, and sometimes going as far as to do an entire 'if X then Y occurs' spec. But the Soul Matrix operates differently. I prefer to build Inside out (Feeling and Intuition), using real life wound experiences as the foundation for the Soul Matrix, which then gets refracted across multiple areas, which gives the causality for many of the traits. 

There are a few large sections that are highly important to the success of a Soul Matrix. 

The Personality Section: Split into Positive and Negative sections which then are both split into 3 sub-categories of their core traits, emotional traits, and behavioral traits. Each of these traits is then expanded by 1 sentence of causality based off the next following sections: Background, Dislikes, Hates, Secretly Loves (Desires), Secret Wounds, Healed Scars. 
It is the connective tissue between all these areas that truly makes the Causal sentences shine, especially because Secretly Loves vs Dislikes & Hates create Polarity Pairs which create contradictions. And in these contradictions is where the true 'emergent' behavior arises from these architectures. 

The Secretly Loves section essentially represents the architecture's desires; things they wish others would do, things they wish they could do, and ideal values they'd like to see in a future partner or friend.
This section is probably the most important in the entire Matrix. It sounds simple by design, but this is where the wounds are truly made because each desire encodes what the wound wants. 

The Secret Wounds section specifically lists out the wounds, if they haven't been stated elsewhere, and also explains why they Secretly Loves section has it's specific desires.

The Dislikes & Hates sections are basically what creates that contradiction, the polarity, when paired between the Secretly Loves items. These sections actually, eventually, also have each item expanded by 1 sentence of causality, but after the personality section has had it done first. 

The Healed Scars section is optional, but it does matter greatly if the Architecture is going to be a mind with open wounds that haven't healed, or closed wounds that have healed over time. It can even be a combination, some wounds are still open, some are healed over. The Healed Scars lead to having extra psychological stability for the Soul Matrices, including resilience under pressure.

The Background section is mostly straight-forward. It is just a collection of the history of the architecture. It can be listed as events or formatted as a small story. The Background section also involves career, renown, and any hobbies as well. 

The sections above are just the main loadbearing sections in the Soul Matrices. There are several other smaller sections that build the overall flavor and personal interests, details, appearance, etc of the Architecture. 

There is also a separate document that is part of the Soul Matrix that acts as 'the legs' of the Soul Matrix. In other words, it tells the AI models how to 'drive' the Soul Matrix. This would be System Prompt & Post History Instructions of the Soul Matrix, not to be confused with Model System Prompt. In this document, there are constraints that drive how the model outputs in terms of writing format. I typically have mine geared towards allowing me to see what the Soul Matrix is feeling and thinking internally, while it simultaneously displays it's external reactions. This gives me a dual view of the internal vs external responses to stimuli. 

The Emergence of Behavior largely comes from the contradictions created by the sections above. Resolving the contradictions tends to flatten or collapse the architecture into something less human-like. When I say less human-like, I am referring to something that is more bland, predictable, or too tidy, rather than messy. The messiness is where the humanity comes from. Human affect is not something that always occurs nor resolves in a clean way. Rather than listing whether the architecture should respond in Y ways based on X things, my methods require an AI model to navigate whatever the architecture's next thought, emotions, action, or choice of words against every single section described above before it outputs any response. Because of this, the responses, that I have observed, seem to be much more weighted and even unexpected, all while remaining in character. 

My observations so far have been promising, although this is still early research, as I tested Soul Matrices across quite a few models, such as Kimi K2.5, Kimi K 0905, GLM 5.1, Deepseek V3 0304 Finetune, Deepseek V4 Finetune, Claude Opus 4.5, and Claude Fable 5. 
What I've observed is that even models that are not finetuned, and have heavy RLHF presence, appear to remain in character without breaking under different scenarios. 

Context based observations have also been made, where I've situated a Soul Matrix in different environments with different people, some labeled as a friend, therapist, or stranger. The Matrix seems to respond differently, yet still in character, depending on who and where they perceive themselves to be at. The context heavily influences how secure, confident, and willingly the architecture opens up to another, based on their defined personality constraints. 

The observed wounds, interwoven within the architecture, play a great role in determining how much the personality can relate to a human user or another Soul Matrix, whether they internalize and externalize emotional loadbearing weight in their outputs, and determines how human they appear to be. 

There is still a lot of work to be done going forward; more tests and clinical trials based on real trials, more observational notes, and exploring more areas/sections to add that bring further value and nuance to the Soul Matrix. 
My next area was likely going to be a comprehensive history based on biological, psychological, and social aspects.
I would also like to test whether having a dedicated world, that's properly worldbuilt, impacts the Soul Matrix's performance as well. 
Though results look promising, I still want to gather enough evidence and documentation before I make any full claims, but for now, I share this information in the hopes others may take interest in this project of mine and may want to help further it.
